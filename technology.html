Technology

Blade-referenced camera geometry.

Revelascope holds the retractor blade tip in the camera's frame as a fixed visual reference horizon. The focal point is set just distal to the tip, and the optical axis diverges from the blade at a defined angle — a geometry that gives the surgeon monocular depth perception during open dissection, rather than a flat, ambiguous view.

A retrograde tooth design stabilizes both the retraction and the visual field it produces, aimed at reducing the aggressive, trauma-inducing retraction that a poor view otherwise forces.

Intellectual property

Owned by the University of Chicago and licensed exclusively to Crescata through the Polsky Center's UCGo! pathway. A provisional patent application was filed February 25, 2026 (USPTO 63/990,681, "Surgical Retractor Systems with Integrated Imaging"), covering the blade-referenced camera geometry and the retrograde tooth design. A non-provisional utility application is planned for February 2027, followed by international filing under the PCT. A continuation-in-part application is planned to capture refinements from ongoing prototyping.
