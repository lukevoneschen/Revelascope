A complete end-to-end surgical retraction platform

Visualization throughout the entire nipple-sparing mastectomy procedure.

Expand patient candidacy

Breast size, oncologic disposition, and ptosis currently narrow who can be offered NSM, in part because limited visibility compounds the risk of deep dissection. Clear visualization throughout the cavity widens the eligible population.

Improve patient outcomes

The operative field is displayed on an external monitor, so the surgeon, assistant, and OR team follow the same view rather than competing for sight lines through a small incision. Retraction becomes responsive rather than maximal, reducing the sustained flap tension implicated in necrosis.

Protect surgeon careers

Operating from a monitor removes the static cervical and shoulder loading that line-of-sight dissection imposes, allowing a neutral upright posture throughout the case and addressing a principal driver of occupational musculoskeletal injury.

Integrate seamlessly

Same incision, same dissection plane, same instruments, same haptic feedback. Revelascope augments open NSM at the instrument level rather than asking surgeons to learn a different operation.
