The Vision

A complete end-to-end surgical retraction platform

Visualization throughout the entire nipple-sparing mastectomy procedure — not a new operative paradigm, but the existing one, seen clearly.

Expand patient candidacy

Patients with larger or more ptotic breasts are frequently excluded from NSM, in part because limited visibility compounds the risk of aggressive dissection. Clear visualization deep in the cavity widens who can be offered the procedure.

Improve patient outcomes

When the assistant can see the dissection plane, retraction becomes responsive rather than brute. Reducing sustained flap tension targets the mechanism behind skin-flap and nipple necrosis directly.

Protect surgeon careers

Reliance on a monitor rather than the incision lets the surgeon stand upright. Removing the postural demand of line-of-sight dissection addresses the injury pattern that ends careers early.

Integrate seamlessly

Same incision, same dissection plane, same instruments, same haptic feedback. Revelascope augments open NSM at the instrument level rather than asking surgeons to learn a different operation.
