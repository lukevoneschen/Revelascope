Better surgery starts with better visualization.

Revelascope is a video-enabled surgical retractor integrating a camera and illumination directly into the retractor blade, developed for nipple-sparing mastectomy.

Revelascope is an investigational device under development by Crescata Surgical. Not yet evaluated or cleared by the FDA. Not available for clinical or commercial use.

The Problem

Patient retraction trauma 5–14% skin-flap necrosis Tap to flip ↻

In order for the surgeon to achieve direct line-of-sight visualization, an assistant holds sustained tension on the skin flap. This forceful retraction is a recognized driver of skin flap and NAC necrosis, leading to post-operative complications.

Surgeon injury >50% report career-affecting pain Tap to flip ↻

Dissecting with direct line-of-sight visualization forces non-anatomic and painful operating posture. Over the course of a surgical career, the cumulative physical strain causes chronic injury and early attrition.

Surgical education Only one person can see the field Tap to flip ↻

The dissection happens through a small incision in a deep, dark cavity, so only the operating surgeon has a line of sight. Residents and fellows can't see what the attending sees, which makes the procedure difficult to teach and slow to learn.

Sources: Matsen et al., Ann Surg Oncol 2016; Epstein et al., JAMA Surg 2018; Davis et al., J Surg Res 2014; Hallbeck et al., Ann Surg Oncol 2020.
