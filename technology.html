Technology

Blade-referenced camera geometry.

Revelascope holds the retractor blade tip in the camera's frame as a fixed visual reference horizon, with the optical axis diverging at a defined angle — giving the surgeon monocular depth perception during open dissection. A retrograde tooth design stabilizes both the retraction and the resulting visual field.

Intellectual property

Owned by the University of Chicago, licensed to Crescata via the Polsky Center's UCGo! pathway. Provisional patent filed Feb 25, 2026 (USPTO 63/990,681). Utility filing planned Feb 2027, PCT to follow.
