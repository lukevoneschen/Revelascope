The Plan

A staged path from prototype to clearance

Each stage is built around a working device and validated against practicing breast surgeons before the next begins.

Nov 2025
Alpha prototype First working device. Validated in a cadaveric nipple-sparing mastectomy, where a fellowship-trained breast surgical oncologist completed bilateral NSM using Revelascope as the sole retractor — one side through a standard 10 cm incision, one through a deliberately shortened 6.5 cm incision.
Aug 2026
Alpha-plus prototype Developed in partnership with Ontogen MedTech: single-use disposable device architecture, OEM electronics sourcing and testing, and focal management and depth perception refinement.
Jan 2027
Beta prototype A near-final device architecture, followed by a structured multi-surgeon cadaveric usability study and regulatory strategy work toward design freeze and FDA submission.

Revelascope is an investigational device under development by Crescata Surgical. Not evaluated or cleared by the FDA. Not available for clinical or commercial use.
